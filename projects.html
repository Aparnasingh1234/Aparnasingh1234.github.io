📊 Diversification of Household Savings

Research Project

Studying the diversification of Indian household savings into bank deposits, mutual funds, SIPs, and small savings schemes.

Focus: Monetary Economics, Financial Data, Economic Research

📈 Gold Price Pass-Through

Research Interest

Exploring how changes in gold prices may affect retail jewellery prices in India.

The project considers the possibility of asymmetric price adjustment and the use of time-series econometric methods.

Focus: Time Series, NARDL, Price Transmission
